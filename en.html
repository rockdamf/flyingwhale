Flying Whale is a brand communication studio. We plan and run communities, events, education, talks, camps, workshops and tours so brands connect wider and deeper with their customers and people. The goal: turn those connections into the brand's presence. It's led by Youngsun Paek (Rory), author of “Community Builders.”

Team workshops & team-building, the Seongsu Insight Tour (walkshop), talks on leadership, personal branding and customer experience, conference & event planning, and community building. Everything is designed for the audience — new hires, promotees, executives or teams.

A different kind of team workshop: walking trend districts like Seongsu and Bukchon to read brands, customer experience and space. Executives and teams from Samsung, Hyundai Motor, LG and KT&G have joined — insight from the field, not a classroom.

Tours work best with 6–30 people (executive or team size). Talks and workshops run from small groups to 100+, by arrangement.

Tours are based in Seoul (Seongsu, Hannam, Seochon, Yeonnam, Dosan, Bukchon, etc.), with other regions on request. Talks and workshops are available nationwide and online/hybrid.
